3. Architectural Case Studies & Problem Solving

Case Study 1: Incremental Legacy Modernization Without Downtime

The Challenge: Transitioning a massive production codebase away from legacy jQuery and server-rendered templates across high-traffic consumer webshops while keeping live operations stable for millions of users.

My Mindset & Approach: Incremental modernization rather than risky "big-bang" rewrites. I removed legacy jQuery dependencies piece by piece, rewrote key scripts in clean Vanilla JavaScript for optimal performance, and built complex interactive UI flows as isolated React/TypeScript applications embedded directly into existing pages.

The Result: Continuous stack modernization, significantly lighter front-end bundles, and reduced technical debt without interrupting live business operations.

Case Study 2: Scaling Multi-Brand Design Systems & Accessibility

The Challenge: Ensuring consistent branding, design tokens, and strict accessibility (WCAG) compliance across dozens of distinct partner webshops sharing a single codebase.

My Mindset & Approach: "Fix the root cause, not just the symptom." Instead of writing one-off overrides per page or per shop, I concentrated efforts at the shared core component layer. I utilized design tokens, clearly named component variants, and robust ARIA patterns so that a single fix or improvement instantly propagated across all connected webshops.

The Result: Drastically reduced code duplication, high maintainability, and default compliance with accessibility standards (WCAG 2.1/2.2 AA) for all partner brands.

Case Study 3: Platform-Wide Accessibility (WCAG/EAA) Overhauls at Scale

The Challenge: Fixing uneven accessibility and screen-reader support across thousands of B2C webshops and high-profile partner portals.

My Mindset & Approach: Treating accessibility as a structural default rather than an afterthought. Systematically updating semantic HTML structures, restructuring heading hierarchies (h1-h6), enhancing visible focus states, and integrating rigorous keyboard-navigation and screen-reader reviews into the core code review process.

The Result: Standardized, accessible portals that comply with modern European Accessibility Act (EAA) and WCAG guidelines across all shared components simultaneously.

Case Study 4: Multi-Theme White-Label Architecture & Inheritance

The Challenge: Managing dozens of distinct theme bundles and white-label shops (for sports federations, major sporting events, clubs, venues, and regional ticketing services) without turning the repository into unmaintainable, fragmented forks.

My Mindset & Approach: Leveraging Symfony bundle inheritance and YAML configurations combined with a strict shared-core architecture. Theme-specific bundles were kept thin, overriding only branding and specific options, while core features lived in a unified system.

The Result: An improvement made once automatically benefited over 7,000+ shops, keeping regressions to a minimum and drastically reducing maintenance overhead.
